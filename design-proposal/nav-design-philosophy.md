# Structured Transparency — Design Philosophy

**Form as Language.** The navigation is not a list — it is a spatial grammar. Parent nodes carry weight: a 3-pixel vertical bar of saturated teal anchors them to the left edge, a statement of category identity. Child nodes defer: they recede into the page, reduced in size, indented, prefixed with a quiet circular dot. The eye learns the hierarchy in a single glance, without reading a word.

**Color as Depth.** The primary palette is near-monochrome — white field, graphite ink — with one deliberate accent: #34C6BE, the brand teal. This hue appears only where it earns its place: the accent bars of parent rows, the "NEW" badge, the active-page highlight. Its scarcity gives it authority. Color does not decorate; it signals.

**Scale and Rhythm.** Parent items breathe at 16px / font-weight 600. Child items recede to 14px / font-weight 400. The vertical cadence alternates between two densities — open (parent, 14px gap) and compact (child, 6px gap) — creating a visual pulse that the reader follows without effort. Every measurement is the product of painstaking calibration.

**Spatial Hierarchy.** The 24px left indent on child rows is not decoration — it is a claim of subordination expressed in space. The teal left-border bar on parent rows is not decoration — it is a claim of authority expressed in material. These are not style choices; they are semantic choices rendered visually, with the precision of a master craftsman who has considered every pixel.

**Restrained Craftsmanship.** The work is meticulous in its invisibility: rounded corners at 10px for parents, 8px for children; shadow depths of 1–4px; background tints of rgba(52,198,190, 0.06) for parent hover, rgba(0,0,0,0.02) for child resting states. Nothing is accidental. Nothing shouts. The result is a navigation system that feels inevitable — as though it could not have been designed any other way — which is the highest form of craft.
